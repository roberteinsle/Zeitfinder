# Zeitfinder

Zeitfinder is a fast, mobile-friendly scheduling poll app: an organizer proposes dates or time slots, shares a link, everyone answers **Yes**, **No** or **If need be**, and the organizer fixes the final date – all participants are notified by email with a calendar file.

The user interface is German. This handbook is English.
